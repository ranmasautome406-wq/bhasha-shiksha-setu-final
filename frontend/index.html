About the project

Bhasha Shiksha Setu (SIH26042) is an AI-powered vernacular education platform. It breaks the language barrier in classrooms so every learner understands every concept — in their own language.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is Bhasha Shiksha Setu free?
Yes! The prototype is completely free during the Smart India Hackathon showcase.
Which languages are supported?
English, Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi and Urdu — and more can be added by the admin.
How does the AI Tutor work?
Click the 💬 AI Assistant button on any page, type or speak your question, and choose the language of the answer.
Can teachers create their own lessons?
Yes — teachers can create lessons with text, images, videos, PDFs and publish them instantly to all students.
